Biriyani

Description

Chicken Biryani is a flavorful rice dish made by cooking fragrant basmati rice with tender chicken, aromatic spices, yogurt, and herbs. It is one of the most popular dishes in South Asian cuisine and is loved for its rich taste and irresistible aroma. This simple version is easy to prepare and is perfect for family meals or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil or ghee
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 4 green cardamoms
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Fresh mint leaves, chopped
  • 4 cups water

Steps

  1. Prepare the Base

    2. Heat the oil or ghee in a large pot over medium heat. Add the bay leaves, cardamoms, cloves, and cinnamon, and sauté until fragrant. Add the sliced onions and cook until they become golden brown. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute before adding the chopped tomatoes. Continue cooking until the tomatoes soften and blend into the mixture.

  2. Cook the Chicken

    3. Add the chicken pieces to the pot along with the turmeric powder, red chili powder, biryani masala, and salt. Mix everything well so the chicken is coated with the spices. Stir in the yogurt and cook for 10–15 minutes until the chicken is mostly cooked and the masala becomes thick.

  3. Prepare the Rice

    4. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for about 20 minutes. Drain the rice before using it.

  4. Combine the Ingredients

    5. Add the soaked rice over the cooked chicken mixture. Pour in the water and sprinkle the chopped coriander and mint leaves on top. Gently mix everything together without breaking the rice.

  5. Cook the Biryani

    6. Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes, or until the rice is fully cooked and the water has been absorbed. Turn off the heat and allow the biryani to rest for 10 minutes.

  6. Serve

    7. Gently fluff the biryani with a fork before serving. Enjoy it hot with raita, salad, or pickle.
