Butterchicken

Description

Butter Chicken is a rich and creamy Indian curry made with tender pieces of chicken cooked in a mildly spiced tomato and butter-based sauce. Known for its smooth texture and balanced flavors, this dish is a favorite around the world. It pairs perfectly with naan, roti, or steamed basmati rice and is simple enough to prepare at home.

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • ½ cup fresh cream
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped

Steps

  1. Marinate the Chicken

    2. In a bowl, combine the chicken with yogurt, turmeric powder, red chili powder, and a pinch of salt. Mix well and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes to enhance the flavor and tenderness.

  2. Cook the Chicken

    3. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the marinated chicken and cook for about 8–10 minutes until lightly browned. Remove the chicken from the pan and set it aside.

  3. Prepare the Sauce

    4. In the same pan, melt the butter and sauté the chopped onion until soft and golden. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute before stirring in the tomato puree. Add the coriander powder, cumin powder, garam masala, and salt, then cook until the sauce thickens and the oil begins to separate.

  4. Combine Everything

    5. Return the cooked chicken to the pan and stir well so it is evenly coated with the sauce. Add the fresh cream and sugar (if using), then mix gently. Simmer the curry for 10–15 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked and the sauce becomes rich and creamy.

  5. Finish the Dish

    6. Taste the curry and adjust the seasoning if needed. Garnish with freshly chopped coriander leaves and an extra drizzle of cream for a restaurant-style finish.

  6. Serve

    7. Serve the butter chicken hot with naan, roti, paratha, or steamed basmati rice for a delicious and satisfying meal.
